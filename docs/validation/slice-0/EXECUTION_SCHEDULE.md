# Execution schedule

Five working weeks plus one closing week that holds the decision meeting and the
contingency days, for two founders working part-time on the study alongside everything
else. No spend beyond small thank-you gestures and any existing domain for the static
pages. Dates are relative; fill the calendar when the run is scheduled. The sequence is
binding; the durations are estimates. The schedule is built to reach every minimum in
`README.md` §2a through the buffered targets in `RECRUITMENT_PLAN.md` §0, not the bare
minimums.

## Effort assumptions

| Activity | Founder hours (estimate) |
|---|---|
| Outreach, screening and booking, per contact reached | 0.25 |
| Policy reading per channel, with capture and row | 2 to 3 |
| Structured seller interview (`SI-###`) | 0.5 plus 0.5 write-up and coding |
| Seller workflow session (`WF-###`, intake to publication) | 1.5 plus 1 for draft, check and page |
| Founder item (`FI-###`): facts, photos, listing, page, arm record | 0.5 to 1 |
| Real buyer contact (`RC-####`): conversation, row, summary to seller | 0.25 to 0.5 per contact |
| Visibility checks per live listing (24h, 48h, 7d) | 0.25 total |
| Moderated buyer session (`BM-###`) | 0.5 plus 0.5 write-up |
| Daily second-founder review | 0.5 to 1 |
| Evidence review and memo | 8 to 12 total |

Two founders at 15 to 20 hours a week each give 150 to 200 hours over five weeks and
180 to 240 over six. The plan therefore assumes about 20 hours a week each, or the
sixth week used for sessions. If the founders' available hours are lower, extend the
calendar by whole weeks before anything else: never shrink a cohort, never lower
eligibility, never count one participant in two denominators to close the gap.

## Stop-the-clock rules

- A confirmed hard stop pauses new sessions on the affected workflow the same day.
- If by the end of week 3 fewer than 3 non-founder workflow sellers have started, week 4
  is recruitment, and the decision moves into week 7.
- If by the end of week 3 fewer than 14 interviews are complete, add interview slots in
  weeks 4 and 5 before anything else and extend outreach. A moderated session, a
  workflow debrief or a real buyer conversation is never counted as an interview to
  close the gap.
- If 20 interviews are not complete at the decision meeting, the memo records H-11 as
  inconclusive and `BIZ-092` as unmet; pricing stays unfixed whatever the Slice 0
  decision (`SLICE_0_SCORECARD.md` §5 rule 3).
- Never shorten an observation window to make a date. A workflow whose window has not
  ended is not complete.
- Never move a participant between cohorts to reach a minimum (B-11), and never drop a
  completed record because its result is unfavourable.
