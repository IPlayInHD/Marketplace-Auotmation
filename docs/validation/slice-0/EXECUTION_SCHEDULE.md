# Execution schedule

Five working weeks plus one contingency week, for two founders working part-time on the
study alongside everything else. No spend beyond small thank-you gestures and any
existing domain for the static pages. Dates are relative; fill the calendar when the
run is scheduled. The sequence is binding; the durations are estimates.

## Effort assumptions

| Activity | Founder hours (estimate) |
|---|---|
| Policy reading per channel, with capture and row | 2 to 3 |
| Seller interview | 0.5 plus 0.5 write-up |
| Seller workflow session (intake to publication) | 1.5 plus 1 for draft, check and page |
| Buyer phase operator time per live listing per day | 0.5 to 1 |
| Moderated buyer session | 0.5 plus 0.5 write-up |
| Daily second-founder review | 0.5 to 1 |
| Evidence review and memo | 8 to 12 total |

## Week 1 — Recruitment and preparation

| Day | Founder A (Product) | Founder B (Engineering) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fill the consent script placeholders (contact, gesture); print materials; agree OVQ defaults and record them | Set up the protected store and the ID map; prepare the static-page template outside the repository; verify it contains nothing protected and no third-party script |
| 2 to 3 | Outreach through channels 1 and 2 of `RECRUITMENT_PLAN.md`; screen replies; assign IDs | Policy reading (`MARKETPLACE_FEASIBILITY_PROTOCOL.md` step 1) for the channels sellers name most; evidence rows and captures |
| 4 | Outreach continues; book interviews | Policy reading continues; agree the two primary channels for this run (OVQ-03) |
| 5 | Schedule the pilot session; confirm at least 6 eligible sellers, 3 of them non-founders, and the founders' own 15 to 20 items | Prepare the fact sheets, private sheets, stopwatch logs, scorecard files |

**Exit:** at least 6 eligible sellers (3 non-founder), policy rows for every intended
channel, protected store working, consent materials complete.

## Week 2 — Pilot session and fixes

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| 1 | Pilot: one founder-owned item through the full seller workflow with the other founder as seller-participant; one moderated buyer session with a non-founder buyer |
| 2 | Review: what confused, what leaked, what took too long; fix scripts, page, sheets; log every change |
| 3 | First non-founder seller interview and first workflow session |
| 4 to 5 | Founders' own listings published on the primary channels (all three arms across items: link in body, link only in reply, phone-number control); buyer phase begins; daily review starts |

**Exit:** pilot fixes applied; at least one non-founder workflow live; founder listings
live on both primary channels.

## Weeks 3 and 4 — Main sessions

| Rhythm | Activity |
|---|---|
| Two or three seller sessions per week | Interviews and first workflows; second workflows one week after the first for each seller |
| Every day | Operator answers real buyers on all live listings; buyer scorecard rows; summaries to sellers; 24h / 48h / 7d visibility checks; second-founder review |
| Four or five moderated buyer sessions per week | Reach 20 by the end of week 4 |
| End of week 3 | Checkpoint: count confirmed real contacts; if the pace will not reach 100 by the end of week 4, add founder-owned listings now, not later |
| End of week 4 | All 10 workflows completed or closed; observation windows ended; channel rows classified |

**Exit:** at least 10 non-excluded workflows, at least 100 confirmed real contacts, 20
moderated sessions, every channel row classified with reviewer initials.

## Week 5 — Evidence review and decision

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| 1 | Manifest integrity check; every hash recomputed; every deletion verified |
| 2 | Memo §3 (completeness) and §8 (bias) written and dated by both founders before any result is computed |
| 3 | Scorecard computed per `SLICE_0_SCORECARD.md`; every metric with fraction, percentage and missing-data count; hard-stop review of every critical and major incident |
| 4 | Decision meeting: hard stops → canonical gate → workflow unit → completeness; memo §4 to §7, §9 to §11 |
| 5 | Both founders sign; redacted memo and redacted scorecards committed with manifest hashes; participants thanked and told the study is closed; deletion schedule (90 days) diarised |

## Week 6 — Contingency

Reserved for: recruitment shortfall (extend outreach, not eligibility); marketplace
removals requiring re-placement on another surface; a RERUN decision's re-planning; a
participant withdrawal that drops the non-founder count below 3. If the contingency
week is not needed, the 90-day deletion clock still starts at the signature date.

## Stop-the-clock rules

- A confirmed hard stop pauses new sessions on the affected workflow the same day.
- If by the end of week 3 fewer than 3 non-founder sellers have started, week 4 is
  recruitment, and the decision moves into week 6.
- Never shorten an observation window to make a date. A workflow whose window has not
  ended is not complete.
